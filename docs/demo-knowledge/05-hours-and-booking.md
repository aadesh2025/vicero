# Opening hours and booking policy (DEMO)

## Hours (DEMO)
Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 18:00. Saturday 10:00 to 14:00. Closed Sunday and public holidays. Times are India Standard Time (IST).
The chat assistant is available 24 hours a day; the human team replies during the hours above.

## Booking a call or demo
- Calls and demos last 30 minutes and are free.
- They can be booked on weekdays between 10:00 and 17:30 IST, and Saturday between 10:00 and 13:30 IST.
- To book, the assistant needs: the customer's **name**, an **email or phone number**, and a **preferred date and time**.
  A short note on what they want to discuss helps but is optional.
- The assistant checks the time is free, books it, and sends a confirmation on the same channel the customer used.
- Rescheduling or cancelling: tell the assistant (or reply to the confirmation) at least 2 hours before the booked time.
- If the requested time is not free, offer the nearest free times instead of refusing.

## Reminders
A reminder message is sent the day before a booking.
