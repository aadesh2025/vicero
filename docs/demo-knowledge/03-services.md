# Services

## 1. Chat assistant setup
We build your assistant, load your business information, design the website chat widget in your colours, and connect it to the
channels you choose (website, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger). Usually live within a few working days of receiving your information.

## 2. Managed assistant (monthly)
We keep the assistant accurate: updating prices and policies, fixing answers that were wrong, reviewing conversations the assistant
could not handle, and reporting monthly on results.

## 3. Appointment booking automation
The assistant takes bookings in chat, checks free time slots, creates the calendar event, and sends the customer a confirmation on the
same channel they used.

## 4. Custom automations
Connecting the assistant to your other tools: sending leads to a spreadsheet or CRM, notifying your team, order or booking lookups.

## 5. Training and handover
A short session to show your team how to use the Inbox and how to update the assistant's knowledge themselves.
