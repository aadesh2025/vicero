## Two standing rules from the safety track

Repeated in `CLAUDE.md` because they are easy to violate by accident:

1. **A prompt line is not enforcement.** Measured on this stack, prompt-only grounding
   fabricates 12 times out of 15 on `llama-3.1-8b-instant`. Anything that must not happen
   needs a code-level check.
2. **Re-run the no-context A/B** after any edit to `DEFAULT_SYSTEM_PROMPT`,
   `db/templates.py`, or the identity lock in `chat/assembly.py`. A unit test cannot see
   that regression.
