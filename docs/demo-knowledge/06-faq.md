# Frequently asked questions

**What do you sell?**
We set up and run AI chat assistants for businesses: setup, a managed monthly service, appointment-booking automation and custom
automations. See the services and pricing pages.

**How much does it cost?**
Demo plans start at 49 USD a month (Starter), 149 USD (Growth) and 399 USD (Scale), plus a one-time setup fee. These are demo figures;
the team confirms the real price after a short call.

**Which channels do you support?**
Website chat widget, WhatsApp, Instagram DMs and Facebook Messenger.

**Does it replace my staff?**
No. It answers the repetitive questions and takes bookings so your staff can focus on the cases that need a person. A human can take
over any conversation at any time.

**What if the assistant does not know the answer?**
It says it does not have that information and offers to bring in a team member. It does not guess.

**Can it speak other languages?**
It can reply in the language the customer writes in. Your knowledge is best provided in the languages your customers use.

**How long does setup take?**
Usually a few working days after we receive your information.

**Is my customers' data safe?**
Each customer's data is kept separate from other customers' data. We do not sell data. Ask the team for details of retention and
deletion.

**Can I try it first?**
Yes, there is a free trial period (demo: 14 days).

**How do I book a demo?**
Tell me your name, your email or phone number and a preferred day and time, and I will book it.
