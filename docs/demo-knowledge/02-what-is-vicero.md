# What is Vicero?

Vicero is the AI chatbot and automation platform we use to run assistants for our customers.

What an assistant can do:
- Answer customer questions from your own information (price lists, FAQs, policies, product details). It answers only from what
  you have given it, and says so when it does not know instead of guessing.
- Talk on several channels at once: your website chat widget, WhatsApp, Instagram DMs and Facebook Messenger.
- Hand a conversation to a human when it is unsure, when the customer asks for a person, or when the customer is upset. The team sees
  these in a shared Inbox and can reply from there.
- Book appointments: it collects the customer's name, contact details and preferred time, then confirms the booking.
- Remember the conversation so customers do not have to repeat themselves.

What you get in the dashboard: all conversations in one place with the customer's name and photo, an Inbox for chats that need a
person, analytics (how many chats, how many were solved without a human), and a simple editor for the assistant's knowledge and tone.

Privacy: your knowledge and your customers' messages belong to you. Each customer's data is kept separate from other customers' data.
