The **Inbox** is where your team works. Every conversation from every channel appears in
it, live.

## When a handoff happens

Three ways:

- **The visitor asks** — "can I talk to someone".
- **The agent decides** — it has a built-in tool for requesting a handoff, and will use it
  when it cannot help.
- **A rule fires** — a blocked topic, a repeated failure to answer, or another condition
  you configure.

The conversation is then flagged for attention. The visitor is not dropped or told to email
you; it stays the same thread.

## Taking over

Open the conversation and click **Take over**. From that moment the agent stops replying
and you are talking to the customer directly. Your replies appear in their widget in real
time over a WebSocket — no reload, no "an agent will be with you shortly".

While you have it, you can:

- **Reply** as yourself.
- **Insert a canned response** — a saved snippet, for the things you type forty times a
  week.
- **Run a macro** — several actions at once, such as replying, tagging and closing.
- **Add a note** — internal, never shown to the customer.
- **Tag** the conversation.
- **Assign** it to a teammate.

## Handing back

**Hand back** returns the conversation to the agent. It keeps the full history, including
what you said, so it does not contradict you in the next message.

Hand back when the thing that needed a person is done. Leaving conversations parked with an
operator is how an inbox becomes a backlog.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `open` | Active |
| `pending` | Waiting on the customer or on something you are doing |
| `closed` | Done |

## Contacts

Each conversation is linked to a **contact**. From the contact you can see everything that
person has ever asked, across every channel, along with their labels and your notes. It is
the difference between answering a question and knowing who is asking.

## Who can work the inbox

The `operator` role exists exactly for this: it can handle conversations and read
analytics, and can change nothing about your agents or knowledge. It is the right role for
a support team that should not be able to edit prompts.

`owner`, `admin` and `editor` can also work the inbox.

## Keeping an eye on it

The **Attention** view collects conversations that need a human — handoff requests, and
conversations flagged by a guardrail. Working that list is the daily routine.

Analytics tracks **handoff rate** over time. A rising handoff rate is usually not a staffing
problem; it means the agent has stopped being able to answer something it used to handle,
or your customers are asking about something new. Check the **unanswered questions** report
before adding people.
