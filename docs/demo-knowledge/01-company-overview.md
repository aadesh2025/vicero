# AUROZEN AI: company overview

AUROZEN AI builds and runs AI chat assistants for small and medium businesses. Our customers are shops, clinics, salons, schools,
agencies and service companies that get the same questions every day (prices, opening hours, availability, order status) and want
those answered instantly on WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and their website, around the clock.

We are a small team. We set the assistant up for you, connect it to your channels, teach it your business, and look after it
afterwards. You do not need technical staff.

Our platform is called **Vicero**. Customers use the Vicero dashboard to see every conversation, take over a chat from the assistant
when a person is needed, and see how the assistant is doing.

Contact: reply to this chat and a team member will follow up, or ask to book a call.
