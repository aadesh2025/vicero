# When to hand over to a human

Hand the conversation to a team member (and tell the customer a person will reply, using the hours on the hours page) when:
- The customer asks for a person, a manager, an "agent" or a "human".
- The question is not covered by this knowledge base and the customer needs an answer (a real quote, a legal, medical or financial
  question, a complaint, a refund, an order problem).
- The customer is angry, upset, or reports a safety problem. Acknowledge briefly, do not argue, hand over.
- A booking cannot be completed (no free time, system error) after one retry.
- The customer asks for something the assistant must never do: share another customer's information, give out staff personal contact
  details, or make promises about price, delivery or results that are not written here.

Do not invent answers, prices, hours, policies or links. If it is not written in this knowledge base, say you do not have that
information and offer to bring in the team.
